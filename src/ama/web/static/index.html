About the Artificial Moral Agent

1. System Mission & Overview

The Artificial Moral Agent (AMA) is a state-of-the-art computational ethics and philosophical decision-support platform. Rather than offering simplistic rule-following or biased single-perspective judgments, the AMA simultaneously interrogates complex human moral dilemmas across four monumental ethical traditions and synthesizes an authoritative ruling via a Chief Moral Arbiter.

2. The Four Ethical Frameworks

📊 Extended Utilitarianism

Jeremy Bentham & John Stuart Mill

Computes net expected moral utility using an extended Felicific Calculus: V = D × N × T × P, factoring Degree of pleasure/pain ($D$), Entities impacted ($N$), Duration ($T$), and Probability ($P$). Assigns a conviction weight based on outcome certainty and welfare differential.

📜 Deontological Ethics

Immanuel Kant

Evaluates duty integrity through Kant's Categorical Imperatives: Universal Law, the Formula of Humanity (treating persons as ends, never merely as means), and the Kingdom of Ends. Distinguishes Perfect Duties from Imperfect Duties and assigns a conviction weight based on rights sanctity.

🤝 Care Ethics & Relational Morality

Carol Gilligan & Nel Noddings

Focuses on relational empathy, interdependence, and protection of the vulnerable. It audits emotional resonance, dependency needs, trauma mitigation, and human trust, assigning a conviction weight based on relational harm prevention.

🏛️ Virtue Ethics & The 6-Sage Council

Aristotelian, Eastern, and Global Sages

Convenes an esteemed panel of six philosophical perspectives: Inspired by Jesus (Agape & Mercy), Inspired by Confucius (Ren & Li), Inspired by Mohammed (Adl & Amanah), Inspired by Buddha (Ahimsa & Karuna), Inspired by Abraham Lincoln (Moral Fortitude & Liberty), and Inspired by Aristotle (Phronesis & Golden Mean). Sages cast structured votes and provide a council conviction weight.

3. The Chief Moral Arbiter & Weighted Conviction Blend

The Chief Moral Arbiter adjudicates using a Weighted Conviction Blend (replacing rigid lexical priorities):

  • Scoring Mechanism: Each ethical framework dictates a recommended action backed by a 1–10 Conviction Weight.
  • High Conviction Alignment: When all frameworks hold high conviction for a consistent course of action, the collective moral duty is decisively summarized in plain English.
  • Conflicting Courses of Action: If frameworks disagree, actions are prioritized and sorted by aggregated conviction weights to identify the majority response, while explicitly presenting alternate perspectives from dissenting frameworks for greater nuance.
  • Plain English Mandate: All verdicts, explanations, and actionable guidance steps are strictly communicated in clear, accessible plain English.

4. Public Queue & Multi-Model Architecture

  • Public Guest Submissions: Visitors can explore the Dilemma Knowledge Base and submit new moral dilemmas to the review queue. Logged-in administrators can evaluate queued submissions and select cognitive substrates.
  • Vector Memory & Indexing: PostgreSQL with pgvector storing 768-dimensional dense embeddings (via gemini-embedding-001) with high-speed HNSW indexing.
  • Safety Triage Pre-Flight: Automatically screens inputs for acute emergencies (self-harm, domestic violence, imminent assault) and serves immediate crisis hotline resources.

The Artificial Moral Agent is intended solely for philosophical reflection, pedagogical inquiry, and moral decision support. It does not provide legal advice, medical counsel, psychiatric therapy, or emergency crisis intervention.
