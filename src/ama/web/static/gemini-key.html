Get a free Gemini API key

ArtificialMoralAgent uses Google's Gemini Flash-Lite by default. You can get a free key in about a minute, with no credit card, from Google AI Studio.

How to get it

  1. Sign in: go to Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Accept the terms: the first time, accept the terms of service.
  3. Create a key: click Get API key in the sidebar or top menu, then click Create API key.
  4. Copy it: copy the key straight away and keep it private, like a password.

Use it with ArtificialMoralAgent

  1. On the computer running ArtificialMoralAgent, run ama init and paste the key when asked. (Or add GEMINI_API_KEY=your-key to ~/.ama/.env.) Then restart ama serve.
  2. Check it with ama doctor: the model reachable and embeddings lines should say PASS.

Good to know

  • Cost: free. The free tier needs no credit card or billing account.
  • Limits: per-minute and daily request and token limits. The free tier is meant for prototyping and testing, not heavy production use.
Privacy on the free tier: Google may use free-tier inputs and outputs to improve its models. Avoid putting sensitive or private details into dilemmas while using a free key. For private use, use a paid key, or the local preset with Ollama, where nothing leaves your computer.
